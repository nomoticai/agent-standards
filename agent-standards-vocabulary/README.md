# Agent Standards Vocabulary

A recommended taxonomy for engineering accuracy in agentic systems, published deliberately as a living recommendation rather than a standard, following the model SAE used for driving automation: offered for adoption, revised as the field moves, binding on nobody.

## The document

### [AI and Agent Vocabulary](ai-agent-vocabulary.md)

Three parts. **Levels of Agent Automation (L0 through L5)**, the SAE J3016 analog for agents: levels of automation, never levels of autonomy, with authority delegated at every level and no level at which authority originates in the system. **Corrections**, entries for the terms where industry usage and engineering reality diverge: automation and autonomy, agent and macro, search and discovery, catalog and population, findable and available, name and identifier and identity and address, onboarding and registration, connection state and interaction state, declares and attests and judges. **Deprecated usages**, a replacement table for specification authors.

The document cites formal definitions of record (ISO/IEC 22989) where they exist, and states plainly where the field diverges from them.

## Why a recommendation rather than a standard

Vocabulary imposed as a standard gets ignored; vocabulary that survives review gets adopted. The automobile industry's SAE J3016 demonstrated the model: a Recommended Practice whose levels became the industry's shared reference through accuracy rather than mandate. Preference-vocabulary standards without that discipline have a graveyard (P3P, DNT), and this directory takes the lesson.

## How to contribute

Propose terms, corrections, or counter-definitions by issue or pull request, with reasoning stated. One criterion governs adjudication: does the proposed usage make specifications more accurate about where capability, state, and accountability actually live. Disagreements about individual entries are welcome and adjudicated on that criterion alone.

Conventions and style are in the repository [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
